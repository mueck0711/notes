[出典: Bitcoin Magazine](https://bitcoinmagazine.com/news/sec-chair-paul-atkins-supports-clarity-act)

### ⚡ 耐量子署名候補HAWK-256、AIが60時間で解読

[出典: TFTC](https://www.tftc.io/hawk-256-broken-claude-mythos-bitcoin-bip-360-post-quantum)
